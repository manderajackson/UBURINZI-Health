# Closing the team gap

The call requires "a committed founding team". You are currently solo. Two honest ways to fix it in
90 days — both are defensible in the application.

## Option A — Co-founder (best)

| You have | You need | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare IT + clinical workflows | Business development & sales | Clinic management colleague, Kigali MBA network |
| Technical building | Fundraising & partnerships | Someone from an NGO / USAID / Ministry of Health programme |
| Rwanda context | Pan-African perspective | A health-system operator in Uganda, Kenya or DRC |
| Product & data | Clinical credibility at scale | A senior clinician interested in digital health |

Where to meet them, in Kigali: **Norrsken House**, **Impact Hub Kigali**, **timbuktoo HealthTech Hub**
events, **Rwanda Health Tech** meetups, and the University of Rwanda / CMU-Africa networks.

## Option B — Advisory team (faster, and instantly credible)

| Role | Who to ask | What they unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical Advisor | Senior doctor at your clinic | Medical credibility + protocol sign-off |
| Technical Advisor | A senior developer/CTO you know | Depth beyond a solo founder |
| Business Advisor | Someone from Rwanda's startup ecosystem | Go-to-market and fundraising credibility |

## How to write it in the application

> "Uburinzi Health is led by [Your Name], a healthcare IT professional with deep clinical and
> technical expertise, supported by a committed advisory team including [Dr. X, Clinical Advisor] and
> [Name, Business Advisor]. We are actively recruiting a co-founder with business development and
> fundraising experience to join full-time during the incubation period."

Honest, meets the requirement, and shows self-awareness — selection committees respect that.

## Equity / commitment notes

* Advisors: 0.25–0.5 % each, 2-year vest, 1-year cliff, a monthly call and named responsibilities.
* Co-founder: 10–30 % depending on commitment, 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff.
* Write down the responsibilities *before* you hand over equity.
