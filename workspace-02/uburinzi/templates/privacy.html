Uburinzi Health — privacy policy

Last updated {{ today }}. This is the policy linked from our Google Play listing.

What Uburinzi Health is

Uburinzi Health is a clinical follow-up tool used by clinics. It sends short messages (SMS, WhatsApp or a voice call) to patients with long-term conditions — diabetes, hypertension, HIV, TB, asthma, epilepsy, heart failure and antenatal care — between their clinic visits, and records their answers so nurses can act early. It is used by clinic staff, not by the public.

What we collect

  • Patient name, phone number, medical record number, year of birth, sex, village, preferred language, condition and medication.
  • Health responses the patient sends back (for example "1 = I took my medication"), plus appointment attendance.
  • Message logs — what was sent, when, whether it was delivered, and its cost.
  • Staff accounts — name, work email and a hashed password, plus an audit trail of who acknowledged or resolved each alert.

Why we use it

Solely to remind patients about medication and appointments, to detect when someone is struggling, and to alert their clinic. We do not use patient data for advertising, and we never sell it. Messages are clinical, never promotional.

Consent

A patient is only messaged after their clinic has recorded that the patient agreed. Patients can withdraw at any time by replying STOP (or by asking the clinic), and a nurse can switch messaging off for a patient immediately in the app. Opt-out requests are honoured within 24 hours, and every consent decision is stored with a timestamp.

Who can see it

Clinic staff who are logged in, and the technical team that keeps the service running. Messages travel over encrypted connections; provider credentials (SMS, WhatsApp, voice) are stored encrypted; passwords are hashed. We keep data only as long as the clinic needs it for care and audit, then delete it on request.

Children

The service follows the clinic's own patients. Where a patient is a child, the clinic obtains consent from a parent or guardian, as it does for any other care.

Your rights

Patients can ask their clinic to see what is stored about them, correct it, or have it deleted. Under Rwanda's law on the protection of personal data, these requests are handled by the clinic that holds the record.

The app on your phone

The Uburinzi app is the clinic's dashboard, packaged from our website so it can be installed like any other Android app. It requests no camera, microphone, location or contacts — it does not need them, and patients receive ordinary SMS, so they never have to install anything at all.

Contact

Questions, corrections or deletion requests: ask your clinic, or email {{ contact_email }}.
