Integrations & webhooks

Plug Uburinzi into any clinic system.

Inbound SMSPOST /api/webhooks/sms/inbound
Delivery reportPOST /api/webhooks/sms/delivery
Patient upsert (EMR adapter)POST /api/integrations/patients
Lab result triggerPOST /api/integrations/lab-results
Impact reportGET /api/impact-report
Adapters are data-only: a CSV export from ClinicPlus, OpenMRS or DHIS2 is enough to go live. A middleware adapter for live APIs is a ~2-day build per system.
