# 2-minute demo video script

Record at 1440×900, full screen, signed in as `manderajackson99@gmail.com`.
Keep the browser on the Dashboard as your opening frame.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 — The problem

> "In Rwanda, a diabetic patient is diagnosed, given 30 days of medicine, and then the clinic loses
> them for a month. Medication adherence is below 50%. This screen is what that loss looks like from
> the clinic's side: nobody knows who is taking their medicine until they come back sick.
> Uburinzi Health closes that gap — with the phone the patient already has."

## 0:20 – 0:45 — The loop

Go to **SMS Simulator**, click **Generate realistic replies**.

> "Every patient gets a protocol message in Kinyarwanda: *Did you take your medication today?
> Reply 1 for yes, 2 for no, 3 if you're not well.* Watch the dashboard — those replies are arriving
> right now and the adherence number is recalculating."

Back to **Dashboard**, point at the adherence KPI and the WHO baseline comparison.

## 0:45 – 1:10 — The escalation

Go to **Alerts**, sort red first, click into a patient.

> "This patient has missed three doses in a row. The rule engine flagged them red, and the nurse
> sees exactly one thing to do: call them today. Everything else is green and handled automatically —
> that is how one nurse monitors five times as many patients."

Show the conversation thread, then click **Acknowledge**.

## 1:10 – 1:30 — Enrolment in ten seconds

**Patients → Add patient**, fill one row, submit.

> "Enrolment is one form. The welcome SMS goes out immediately in their language, and the scheduler
> picks up the check-in cadence from the national treatment guidelines for their condition."

## 1:30 – 1:50 — Integration and scale

**Import / Export**, then **Settings → Integrations**.

> "A CSV export from ClinicPlus, OpenMRS or DHIS2 is enough to onboard a clinic in an afternoon.
> Inbound replies come through the same webhook Africa's Talking calls — switching from the simulator
> to live SMS is one environment variable. That's why this scales from one pilot clinic to any
> clinic on the continent."

## 1:50 – 2:00 — The ask

> "Fifty-two patients are being monitored in this demo. Next month it's real patients in our pilot
> clinic. What we need from partners is the go-to-market support, regulatory mentorship and investor
> access to take it across East and Central Africa."

---

## Framing tips

* Mention **"no app, no smartphone, no internet"** at least once — it is the sharpest differentiator.
* Keep adherence numbers visible on screen; they are the proof.
* If something is slow, refresh once before recording — the dashboard recomputes live.
